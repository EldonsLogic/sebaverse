# DEV-010 independent production authority review

21 November 2026 · R039/R040/R041/R046 · RISK-F3-004. An independent architecture and security subagent reviewed the proposal and source at `fb92198`. Scope was ten minutes of design review. The concern is forged scan clearance, exposure of another learner’s source, or acceptance of stale output in a future qualified runtime. Current AUTH-013 evidence uses authored PDFs and fake engines. [Proposal](production-authority-proposal.md), [authored evidence](../../development/evidence/dev-010-authored-input.md), [AUTH-013](../../development/APPROVALS.md#auth-013--founder-direct-pypdfium2-text-only-poc-direction-19-november-2026).

**Disposition: revise the proposal before an architecture decision.** Keep verification outside both native engines and authorize access by handing one immutable source to an isolated parser. This gives a compromised parser no signing role. The production risk remains open until a qualified scanner, launcher and isolation boundary demonstrate the protections below. This review records no runtime, ADR or phase approval. AUTH-014’s database supplier wait remains in force. [Risk register](../../registers/decisions-risks-unknowns.md#L120), [ADR-014](../../architecture/adr/ADR-014-minimal-digital-pdf-poc.md), [AUTH-014](../../development/APPROVALS.md#auth-014--recovery-choice--wait-for-supplier-20-november-2026).

## Required proposal revisions

1. **Name the trusted endpoints and what they attest.** State who issues the signed job, which trusted endpoint consumes it, and how the authenticated launch and scan requests derive from it. A broker checking its own signature alone gives no independent authorization evidence. Keep the exact eight-field binding and define its opaque scope digest from broker-owned scope and authorization generation. The scanner-side signer must bind a successful, complete verdict from the approved scanner invocation to the same sealed input and its independently measured count and hash. Resolve scanner identity to the approved executable, rules or signature data, and scan policy. Require the signer to hold on incomplete, skipped, timed-out or otherwise indeterminate scans. If HMAC is retained, name who authenticates and provisions the two distinct keys, who can mint each envelope, and how stale or revoked credentials are rejected. The trusted broker can mint scan HMACs with the verification key. Neither native engine receives keys, and the scanner grant is limited to its measured dispatch input. The existing three-field adapter reply and fixture-key transport supply no production evidence for these roles. Use bounded decoding and exact binding checks before canonicalizing signed fields. [Broker process](../../backend/modules/ingestion/service.py#L138), [fixture verifier](digital_pdf.py#L79), [authored fixture](../../backend/tests/ingestion/fixtures/pdf_worker_fixture.py).
2. **Make source release and replay rules precise.** The broker currently rechecks under the quota/upload/learner locks, exits the transaction, then calls the parser. A revocation can commit in that gap. Define how the trusted launcher releases the descriptor once for the active dispatch, ordered against the same authoritative revocation fence. Cover the corresponding release to the scanner and prove that every relevant scope invalidator participates in the ordering or invalidates the launch before release. Keep storage I/O, scanning and parsing outside long-held database locks. Mark access stale before signalling active work, provide a bounded stop for each engine, close its handles and recheck again before accepting the manifest. Define who tears down an abandoned invocation on broker failure and how the retry obtains a fresh dispatch, source handle and verdict without duplicate acceptance. The existing consent path bumps authorization generation under the learner lock. Scope changes reflected by live reads need their own verified ordering with release. Bytes already read remain exposed to the process until cleanup. [Current lock order](../../backend/modules/ingestion/service.py#L23), [parse and commit fences](../../backend/modules/ingestion/service.py#L138), [withdrawal](../../backend/modules/ingestion/service.py#L233), [consent generation](../../backend/modules/identity/consent.py#L8), [live facts](../../backend/modules/access/service.py#L8).
3. **Anchor expiry to the dispatch.** The fixture verifier checks whether an expiry is within 120 seconds of the verification time. Define a trusted broker deadline when issuing the dispatch so delayed or repeated verification cannot extend its lifetime. Require both envelopes to stay within that deadline, and check the earlier expiry before parser release and while holding the final acceptance fence. Bound staging, scanning, launch, pipes and cleanup with monotonic elapsed-time limits. An authentic duplicate attestation alone cannot obtain a second launch or commit. [Fixture expiry](digital_pdf.py#L87), [existing watchdog](process_guard.py#L246).
4. **Qualify the two-engine handoff, including cleanup.** Require the scanner and parser to receive independently positioned, read-only handles to the same sealed inode, or verify equivalent positional-read behavior. Validate object identity, size, access mode and seals before release. The Linux candidate needs full write, grow and shrink seals and a fixed seal set. A future-write seal alone permits existing writable shared mappings and cannot satisfy the invariant. Keep every other file handle, parent and peer process, private mount, control socket and credential inaccessible. Both native engines need reviewed immutable code and limits on time, memory, CPU and process count, plus a supervisor that closes capabilities and proves process-tree teardown after failure, cancellation and normal exit. A process group and script hash provide only the existing fixture guard properties. Retain quota or an explicit cleanup obligation where staged or partial artifacts can survive failure. Include swap, dumps and diagnostics in that review. [Linux file seals](https://man7.org/linux/man-pages/man2/F_ADD_SEALS.2const.html), [Linux anonymous files](https://man7.org/linux/man-pages/man2/memfd_create.2.html), [Linux cgroup v2 process-tree controls](https://docs.kernel.org/admin-guide/cgroup-v2.html#core-interface-files), [guard contract](process-guard-contract.md).

## Later acceptance evidence

- Race cancellation, consent withdrawal, grant loss and scope drift immediately before scanner and parser release, after release and before manifest acceptance. Include concurrent duplicate verdict delivery, expiry mid-scan and at commit, and crash/retry while an invocation owns its source. Require that prior revocation prevents release, later revocation discards output and triggers teardown, and no stale attempt commits a manifest or leaks quota.
- Reject a wrong, mutable or writable descriptor, a descriptor with an unexpected position, inherited extra handles, a scanner result from an unapproved runtime or policy, and a scan bound to a different source or dispatch. Verify qualified OS enforcement for both engines, including no surviving descendants, no cross-job source access and no retained private payloads in logs or crash artifacts. Exercise a valid current dispatch that reaches only `needs_review` through the existing six-field output provenance and result schema.
- Keep `digital_pdf.run_job` and the authored SDP1 fixture’s signed envelopes, source bounds and explicit guard profile compatible. Any shared extraction logic must leave the fixture’s signed preflight and disabled default factory effective. A qualified production entry point would receive minimal metadata and the one source handle, and the parent would retain the eight-field expected snapshot. Keep the decoder’s six-field result check and the separate rights/extraction approval path. Record the change in verification placement against ADR-014 before production composition. [Fixture entry point](digital_pdf.py#L175), [fixture input tests](../../backend/tests/ingestion/test_input_transport.py), [result decoder](result_transport.py#L57), [review approval](../../backend/modules/ingestion/service.py#L211).

Review used source inspection and primary kernel documentation. Product, native, scanner, database, isolation and fidelity checks were outside this design increment and were not run.
