The project is an AI-native, adaptive tutoring SaaS, initially targeting Egyptian school students, with a long-term international vision.

**Original product vision and requirements**

- Conversational tutoring in Egyptian Arabic, Modern Standard Arabic, and English, using both text and voice.
- Adaptive explanations, interactive lessons, guided problem-solving, exercises, quizzes, mock exams, and personalized exam preparation.
- Persistent learner intelligence: mastery tracking, misconception diagnosis, prerequisite knowledge graphs, learning paths, active recall, spaced repetition, and progress across sessions.
- Three distinct improvement loops: individual student adaptation, system-wide improvement through evaluated feedback, and optional controlled model improvement.
- Built-in, versioned curricula, starting in Egypt and eventually supporting national, language, IGCSE, IB, American, and additional international education systems.
- Student- and parent-uploaded textbooks, notes, worksheets, and custom curricula, with safe ingestion, OCR, curriculum mapping, provenance, permissions, RAG, and document-grounded explanations.
- Student experience, parent dashboard, WhatsApp progress reports, notifications, and future teacher/school functionality.
- Child safety, guardian consent, privacy, secure data isolation, accessibility, and age-appropriate interactions.
- Affordable commercial model for Egypt, with initial pricing hypotheses around EGP 300/month or EGP 300–500 per course/semester, subject to validation.
- Approximately 100 interested prospective users and an initial supervised pilot involving family/friends, subject to appropriate permissions.
- Global-ready architecture without prematurely building every curriculum or feature.

**AI infrastructure requirements**

- Provider-agnostic integration with OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, Google Gemini, Chinese models such as Qwen and DeepSeek, and potentially locally hosted open-weight models.
- A Jev-style decision/classification layer as an experimental option for routing, misconception classification, and instructional decision support. Benchmark against deterministic rules and smaller language models before adoption.
- Model routing, caching, prompt/context optimization, token accounting, voice economics, GPU/self-hosting economics, operational cost modeling, quality evaluations, and usage limits.
- Optimize cost per successful learning outcome, not merely price per token.
- Keep student learning state and deterministic business rules separate from generative model outputs.
