# Eedi, CENTURY and Khanmigo research

Research date: 2026-11-10. Public web documentation and papers only; no authenticated tutoring session, traffic capture, binary inspection, or private prompt inspection was performed. C = confirmed publication/product documentation; V = vendor assertion; I = inference; U = unknown. “Confirmed” establishes what was published, not independent validation of every claim. Source IDs resolve in [11-sources-and-evidence.md](11-sources-and-evidence.md).

## Eedi

**Distinctive mechanism:** teacher-authored diagnostic distractors connect wrong answers to specific misconceptions. The Diagnostic Engine describes predictive models and a misconception graph containing more than 8,000 misconceptions; its claim of useful diagnosis from ten questions is a vendor capability claim, not an independently measured sensitivity guarantee. It markets integration into third-party curricula and LLMs. The mechanism supports a tight error → diagnosis → targeted intervention loop [W01, V/high confidence in documentation].

**Tutoring:** the current product is deliberately constrained and activates after an error, using Socratic dialogue grounded in that diagnosis [W01]. Do not infer a general autonomous multi-agent tutor. Its research organization describes separate learning-design, data-science and evidence teams plus continuous classroom review and human moderation [W03].

**Outcome evidence:** a 29 October 2026 vendor report describes a randomized trial with 1,179 pupils at ten UK schools and ten weeks of lessons. Gemini tutoring drafts were human-reviewed. Immediate analogous-question success was 56–58% with tutoring versus 43% with static content; broad Star Maths scores did not clearly improve. Added personal history did not clearly beat current-question context. Human review approved 94.7% unchanged, but some mathematical errors reached pupils. Exploratory gender differences require replication; 916 pupils completed both standardized tests. A longer trial beginning September 2026 changes supervision to human-on-the-loop, so earlier safety results cannot simply transfer [W02, C/high publication confidence; medium generalization].

**Knowledge state and sequencing:** predictions about unanswered questions and misconception relations are documented [W01]. The deployed mastery equation, BKT/DKT/IRT variant, uncertainty calibration, forgetting scheduler, treatment of inconsistent answers and choice objective remain U. Papers using Eedi datasets do not establish production algorithms. Public research datasets are an exploration resource with separate terms; “available for research” does not grant commercial reuse [W04].

**Experience/distribution:** DQR supports classroom diagnosis using one smartphone; Eedi Tutor supports targeted math intervention. This is relevant to low-device schools [W05]. Current numerical commercial pricing, Arabic/Egyptian dialect support, voice stack/latency, user-upload grounding, WhatsApp reporting, and full Egyptian/IB curriculum mappings were not established. Do not reuse old Eedi Plus consumer pricing as a current engine quote.

**Reproduce:** original misconception taxonomy; item/distractor authoring template; explicit confidence and confirmatory question before assigning diagnosis; constrained intervention; near-transfer question without hints; human-reviewed content and sampled conversation review. Do not copy Eedi questions, misconception library, graph or models.

## CENTURY Tech

**Distinctive mechanism:** a content graph, user-activity graph and knowledge-tracing predictor inform the recommendation engine. The activity graph includes responses, time and navigation; predicted next-step success guides progression/support [W06, V/high documentation confidence]. No credible inspected evidence identifies BKT, DKT, reinforcement learning or a particular neural architecture. In-house model training on historical responses is documented, but continuous autonomous model updates are not [W07].

**Initial diagnosis:** topic assessments link questions to prerequisite nuggets. A diagnostic generally takes 10–15 minutes, provides an “I don’t know” option and withholds correctness feedback and final score. Teachers can assign diagnostics at topic start/end and deliberately reset them; the system does not automatically reset across differing school calendars [W08, C/high]. These are useful assessment-design decisions: an explicit abstention option avoids forced guessing, while repeated assessment requires awareness of item exposure.

**Progress and review:** recommendation categories distinguish remediation from stretch. Student/teacher dashboards expose attempts, response details and time; sparse activity may produce no recommendations yet [W09]. Memory Boost supplies spaced retrieval; the recommended path interleaves topics and can link prerequisite knowledge across subjects [W10, V/high]. Exact decay functions, uncertainty bounds, mastery thresholds and inconsistent-performance policies remain U.

**Teacher control:** assignments let teachers set shared homework or topic diagnostics to bring adaptive pathways into the current scheme of work [W07]. Recommendations complement human intervention; a teacher override is a reproducible feature rather than proof that algorithmic choices are optimal.

**Tutoring/orchestration:** its evidenced core is adaptive selection of expert-authored micro-lessons, not unconstrained LLM conversation. Retrieval implementation, multi-agent topology, long-term conversational memory and voice architecture are U. An absence of disclosure is not evidence of absence. Its content-production process should be investigated separately from its learner model.

**Commercial scope:** the home product advertises English, math and science for ages 7–16, a seven-day trial, and an annual-plan equivalent starting at £8.33/month; entrance preparation starts at £20.66/month. International navigation advertises English national curriculum and IB, but subject/qualification availability must be checked against exact courses. School/tutoring pricing was not numerically established [W11, V/high as a dated advertised starting price].

**Evidence limits:** its impact page summarizes usage associations in school/Trust studies, including correlations with SATs and international progress [W12]. High-usage students may differ in motivation, teaching, prior attainment or school support. These summaries do not establish a randomized causal advantage of the proprietary recommendation model over a simpler mastery rule.

**Reproduce:** curriculum graph with provenance; diagnostic-to-prerequisite links; several reason-coded recommendation queues; low-data “insufficient evidence” state; spaced review plus interleaving; teacher override and student-visible progress. Do not start with a graph database: relational edge tables suffice for a pilot (I/design recommendation).

## Khan Academy / Khanmigo

**Product/distribution:** guided tutoring integrates with Khan Academy content; teacher tools include lesson planning, rubrics, exit tickets and summaries. Learner access below age 18 requires parent enablement or an institutional partnership. Individual subscriptions require US residency/billing; this is a material Egypt access constraint [W13]. The public consumer page lists $4/month or $44/year before sales tax; institutional rates are quoted separately, and teacher access should be verified by country [W14]. This nonprofit price is not evidence of sustainable unit economics for a commercial startup.

**Mastery and spaced review:** Khan Academy's published Mastery Challenges use six questions across three previously practiced skills. Two correct answers move a skill up; two incorrect move it down; mixed answers leave the level unchanged. Eligibility includes prior practice and a twelve-hour interval [W15, C/high]. This establishes interpretable rules, not BKT/DKT. Course mastery and conversational tutor behavior are separate systems.

**Public technical architecture, 2026:** a Khan-authored AIED paper discloses chat workflows with prompts, moderation and configurable components; Go plus a Python NumPy/SymPy math service; logged component entry points; offline evaluations and Growthbook experiments. It reports injecting mastery/prerequisite state and attempt history. A concise math-agent response reduced median full-response latency by 32.7% in an experiment; deterministic subject routing replaced a more complex classifier alternative. Quality measurement includes independent next-item correctness, engagement, math error, answer disclosure and latency. Limitations include single-turn offline evaluation and a math-error judge with 0.41 false-negative rate [W16, C/high technical disclosure; not a long-term learning RCT].

**Prompt/content orchestration:** its older engineering account describes integration with course, language and learner context; iterative prompting; persona tests and moderation [W17, dated 2023]. Do not treat its then-current model choices as current production. The exact private prompts, full workflow code, all retrieval rules and current per-feature model allocation remain U.

**Safety/parents:** current help documentation specifies text/image moderation and adult notifications, whose recipient depends on family versus school access; district administrators may receive credible-harm alerts if enabled. Parents can review flagged chats. The vendor explicitly acknowledges imperfect detection and says flagged images are represented by generated descriptions rather than stored images [W18, C/high]. This is a useful routing pattern; copying every chat to a parent is not automatically the right privacy policy for an Egypt pilot.

**Voice/Arabic:** published speech output reads the tutor's own text after generation, with four optimized languages: English, Spanish, Hindi and Brazilian Portuguese [W19]. This is evidence of TTS, not a measured full-duplex interruption architecture. Arabic is listed among text response languages, but only those four languages are fully quality-tested [W20]. Egyptian dialect, code-switching, dialect speech accuracy, RTL math rendering, latency and barge-in performance remain unverified.

**Experience/evidence limits:** structured exercises coexist with outside-problem assistance; a Socratic goal is not a guarantee against answer leakage. No product session was observed here. Engagement and immediate next-item outcomes do not establish durable retention, exam transfer or the effect of Khanmigo alone separate from the surrounding content platform.

**Reproduce:** bounded teaching workflows; explicit exercise state; deterministic grading and math checks; separate tool outputs from learner-facing explanations; prompt/component versioning; human-calibrated evaluation; short responses; model routing only after measurement. Original implementation can use SymPy/NumPy under their licenses without copying Khan's private code.


# Research profiles: Synthesis, Duolingo, Squirrel AI

Research cut-off: 10 November 2026. Public documentary research only; no authenticated tutoring sessions, network interception, or binary analysis were performed. Confirmed below means documented in the cited source, not independently measured in this investigation. Vendor claims are identified separately. Historical implementation disclosures must not be assumed to describe every current deployment.

## Synthesis Tutor

**Positioning and observed public offer — high confidence for publication, medium for actual checkout.** The current Tutor page targets ages 5–11 and K–5 mathematics. Its extracted page contains overlapping offers: family $29/month or $119/year for up to seven children; Starter $119/year, Pro $239/year, and Live $480/month for six human sessions. Pro lists placement checks, a planner, session summaries and weekly emails. These coexist in the same page; treat price as an observed advertised offer requiring checkout verification, not a universal current tariff. School enquiries use contact sales. [A01](https://www.synthesis.com/tutor)

**Learning behavior — vendor description, medium confidence.** The company describes expert-designed lessons, ongoing micro-assessment, mastery before progression, interactive manipulatives, read-aloud and adjustable voice speed. These support a constrained interactive teaching experience rather than proving unrestricted conversation. Its public explanation does not disclose the knowledge-state equations, misconception taxonomy, prerequisite graph, forgetting model or how contradictory answers are weighted. BKT, DKT, IRT and reinforcement learning are all **unknown**, not interchangeable explanations of “adaptive.” [A02](https://www.synthesis.com/about)

**Evidence caveat.** A 26 August 2024 company article invokes DARPA Digital Tutor results and a partnership with the original team. Those results concern a different program and population; they cannot establish Synthesis K–5 learning gains, retention or transfer. No independent Synthesis outcome trial was established in this research. [A03](https://www.synthesis.com/blog/does-the-synthesis-tutor-get-results)

**Architecture inference — medium confidence.** A plausible functional decomposition is educator-authored activity → interaction evaluator → remediation/advance decision → visual/audio presentation → progress record. This is an original abstraction of the product description, not a recovered backend architecture. Student personalization is described; aggregate quality review is claimed; autonomous online model training is not established. Agent count, tools, structured output validation, retrieval, curriculum-upload grounding, uncertainty escalation, speech stack, interruption handling, latency, Arabic and Egyptian curriculum alignment remain unknown.

**Reproducible opportunity.** Build original manipulatives and explicit micro-checks; preserve the difference between a child completing a screen and demonstrating transfer. Parent summaries should explain the assessed skill and evidence. Do not copy its art, lessons, protected prompts or interaction code. A narrow coherent lesson can be more useful than a broad unstructured chat.

## Duolingo / former Max tier

**Current packaging correction — high confidence.** The Q3 2026 shareholder letter announces that Max will stop accepting new subscriptions in November 2026. Unlimited Video Call is moving into Super; new Super subscribers already receive access, with rollout to all existing Super subscribers targeted for year-end. Existing individual Max users are to move to Super family at their existing price. Therefore a matrix saying “Video Call requires Max” is already stale. The company also reports declining AI unit costs, without disclosing enough here to reproduce its full cost base. No Egypt-specific current checkout price was verified. [A04](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1562088/000162828026069474/q3fy26duolingo9-30x26share.htm)

**Mastery/prediction mechanism — documented historically, high confidence.** The engineering authors describe Birdbrain V1 as logistic regression inspired by IRT, with learner ability and exercise feature difficulty updates. Birdbrain V2 uses an LSTM and a 40-dimensional learner state updated from exercise history; near-real-time updates and streamed partial-lesson events address missing data from abandonment. Deployment followed shadow predictions and controlled experiments. The reported learning proxy was advancement to harder material, alongside engagement; neither alone proves durable transfer. This is a 2023 account of the 2022 transition, not certification of the November 2026 architecture. [A05](https://spectrum.ieee.org/duolingo)

**Forgetting and reproducibility.** The 2016 HLR paper estimates recall decay and reports improved prediction and engagement relative to its tested baselines. These are language-practice results, not evidence that this exact scheduler remains deployed everywhere or beats simple schedules in a small Egyptian mathematics pilot. [A06](https://research.duolingo.com/papers/settles.acl16.pdf) The MIT-licensed public repository supplies code/data pointers and a concrete event vocabulary: recall, elapsed time, user, lexeme, prior exposures and prior correct responses. Student-data reuse requires its own appropriate permissions even where code is open. [A07](https://github.com/duolingo/halflife-regression)

**Conversation orchestration — explicit first-party disclosure.** Designers constrain Lily by CEFR level, character and conversation stages. A separate generation prepares the first question; mid-call checks permit student topic changes. Post-call extraction appends personal facts to a list supplied on the next call. This is evidence of staged calls and external memory, not autonomous agent swarms or weight updates. The transport, current model, speech recognition, TTS, latency and tool contract are undisclosed here. [A08](https://blog.duolingo.com/ai-and-video-call/)

**Recovery and voice.** A 2025 product update documents captions, post-call feedback and push-to-talk to reduce premature interruption. This makes deliberate turn-taking a useful low-complexity baseline for a child voice tutor. It does not establish full-duplex performance, Arabic voice support or code-switching quality. [A09](https://blog.duolingo.com/product-highlights/)

**System improvement.** Duolingo describes using challenge-response events to model grammar proficiency and extensive A/B testing for personalization changes. [A10](https://blog.duolingo.com/unique-engineering-problems/) Its 2023 content-generation account describes human specialists shaping prompts and reviewing learning content; it supports a curated content-production loop, not unreviewed self-training. [A11](https://blog.duolingo.com/large-language-model-duolingo-lessons/)

**Outcome evidence.** A 2026 company summary reports a one-month comparison among intermediate Japanese speakers learning English, with stronger standardized speaking improvement for frequent Lily use; it also summarizes beginner and Spanish studies. The summary does not establish all randomization, attrition or dosage details, so do not quote it as definitive causal evidence across children, subjects or Arabic. [A12](https://blog.duolingo.com/video-call-research-report/)

**Functional map and limits.** Structured path + response predictor + practice selector + bounded conversational practice + cross-session memory is a supported composite, though the integration contracts are unknown. Misconception-level diagnosis, prerequisite graph semantics, curriculum uploads and school exam alignment are not demonstrated by these sources. Streaks/XP/leaderboards motivate practice but are not mastery measurements; the founder should optimize retention of knowledge and healthy voluntary return, not uninterrupted screen time. No evidence justifies copying Duolingo-scale infrastructure into a pilot.

## Squirrel AI

**Product cycle — vendor documentation, medium confidence.** The current public account describes diagnosis of missing foundations, personalized paths, ability-matched practice, reassessment, and human supervisor support; reports expose movement across a knowledge graph. Consecutive success accelerates progression and repeated errors prompt explanations. This documents claimed behavior, not the numerical update equations. Cross-session tracking is described; a validated forgetting scheduler is not. [A13](https://squirrelai.com/how-it-works/)

**Regional offer and costs.** The current North American FAQ specifies PreK–5 mathematics for the US, center-level enrollment fees rather than one public global tariff, and a locally operated company licensing Chinese technology. Its claim that US personal data stays within the US is a policy representation, not an audited technical finding. Chinese product breadth must not be projected onto the US offer or Egypt. [A14](https://squirrelai.com/faqs/)

**A technically meaningful disclosed subsystem.** The VATE paper describes draft-image analysis followed by structured error analysis and guided dialogue. Matching a question ID plus wrong answer can reuse a cached error diagnosis; new cases go through multimodal analysis. Authors report 78.3% expert approval on 420 selected difficult/common-error cases. They acknowledge that identical wrong answers may need extra intermediate-step keys on harder tasks. This is deployment evidence for one error-analysis subsystem, not a BKT/DKT disclosure for the whole platform. The paper’s multi-agent terminology refers to this workflow; it does not justify an agent for every tutoring function. [A15](https://arxiv.org/html/2409.09403v2)

**Outcome evidence — limited generalization.** The 2019 company-authored preprint compares Yixue against expert classroom mathematics teaching and BOXFiSH English instruction, reporting favorable test performance. This establishes a research claim in those settings, not universal superiority or outcomes of the current LLM tutor. [A16](https://arxiv.org/abs/1901.10268) A separate published article reports randomized eighth-grade Chinese comparisons against large- and small-group expert instruction. Abstract-level access supports the direction of reported results; this review did not verify all full-text attrition and allocation details, so effect sizes are not reproduced. [A17](https://doi.org/10.1080/10494820.2020.1808794)

**Architecture inference and unknowns.** A functional sketch is knowledge-point graph → diagnostic item selection → learner state → targeted content/practice → reassessment, with a VATE error-analysis branch and human supervisor. Exact mastery model, prerequisite edge authoring, inconsistent-response treatment, long-term memory implementation, current speech architecture, Arabic support, upload rights, prompt versions, rollback practices and model-training permissions remain unknown. Historical conference material mentioning algorithm families was found in search but redirected on opening; it is insufficient to assert the present engine uses a specific hybrid.

**Original engineering lessons.** Capture worked steps before labeling misconceptions; require a corroborating probe before persisting a strong diagnosis. Reuse teacher-approved error explanations with question/content versions, but key caches by meaningful solution traces where answers are ambiguous. Never assume one wrong answer has one cause. Retain human review for novel patterns. Cached error analysis and supervised feedback pools are a promising system-improvement mechanism; they do not require online self-training.


# Additional global providers and reproducible patterns

Research date: 10 November 2026. Public documents and repositories were inspected; no student accounts, paid sessions or private APIs were used. Confirmed means the source explicitly describes a capability, not that its educational effectiveness was independently demonstrated. H/M refer to confidence in the specific claim. Source IDs resolve in [11-sources-and-evidence.md](11-sources-and-evidence.md).

## Japan: COMPASS / Qubena

**Distinct contribution:** structured, textbook-linked practice with fine-grained error remediation and short revision sessions, rather than general conversation. The official service page describes analysis of answer content/process, answer time, hint-viewing duration/count and learning history. It claims selection from finely graded “nano-step” questions, handwriting, construction/graph tools, teacher workbooks and real-time analytics. This is vendor disclosure (H for advertised features, M for operation), not proof of a named KT model. The product targets elementary/junior-high schooling in Japan; no evidence here establishes Egyptian curricula or Arabic. [G01](https://qubena.com/service/qubena)

The learner manual documents four mastery bands based on correctness and response time, and automatic return to an earlier stumbling point after error. Its five-minute review considers previously attempted/related questions at least 24 hours after the last attempt. These are unusually concrete operational clues. They support reproducing explicit practice/review modes; they do not expose a forgetting equation or validate diagnosis accuracy. Teacher visibility includes work and notes, useful for separating wrong mathematics from input errors. [G02](https://navi.qubena.com/file_pdf/2163/)

**Unknown:** exact initial-placement method, parameter learning, graph construction, BKT/DKT/IRT use, conversational memory, voice latency, LLM provider, model training loop and public fixed institutional price. Next authorized observation: solve a misconception probe, a careless-slip variant and an untimed equivalent; compare remediation while controlling hints and timing. Such an experiment would examine behavior, not identify source algorithms.

## South Korea: Riiid / Santa

**Distinct contribution:** detailed interaction instrumentation and a substantial public research trail in test preparation. The historical EdNet README describes answer changes, submission, item bundles, explanations and lectures. It explicitly associates daily recommendations with collaborative-filtering correctness probabilities, and adaptive offers with thresholds on wrong answers or falling tag-level accuracy. These claims describe the recorded historical Santa system; they must not be promoted to the current proprietary implementation. [G03](https://github.com/riiid/ednet)

The SAINT research paper presents a Transformer separating exercise and response streams in encoder/decoder, with improved next-response prediction on its study dataset. This is strong evidence of a research method, not evidence that every Santa production decision uses SAINT or that prediction gains cause learning gains. [G04](https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.07033)

EdNet’s 2019 paper releases data under **CC BY-NC 4.0 for research**: public availability does not authorize commercial model training. Use it for permitted research subject to the license and obtain permission before commercial reuse. [G05](https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.03072)

Older official support describes a short level test, weakness-targeted questions, review, reports and personalized TOEIC curricula. It does not establish current prices, K–12 curriculum support or Arabic conversation. Its score-improvement statement is vendor-reported and lacks a causal comparison in that page. [G06](https://support.riiid.co/hc/en-us/articles/4402895958041-Riiid-Tutor-is-released-Try-it-for-free-now-21-06-28)

**Reproduce:** an event schema that records first/final answer, hints and explanation consumption separately. **Do not infer:** time spent reading is learning; next-answer correctness is durable mastery; a TOEIC predictor transfers to Egyptian school mathematics. Exact current agent design, long-term memory and voice are unknown.

## United States: ALEKS / McGraw Hill

**Distinct contribution:** the clearest explicit commercial connection to a formal prerequisite-sensitive assessment theory in this group. ALEKS officially identifies Knowledge Space Theory / Learning Spaces as its foundation: feasible sets of mastered problem types define knowledge states and a ready-to-learn frontier. The site describes adaptive assessment using approximately 20–25 questions. This is an explicit algorithm-family disclosure, unlike generic “AI personalization”; efficiency and precision remain provider claims on that page. [G07](https://www.aleks.com/about_aleks/research_behind)

McGraw Hill describes periodic reassessment and teacher views of readiness and risk. [G08](https://learn.mheducation.com/aleks.html) This supports a design with occasional independent retention checks and prerequisite readiness, rather than treating all historical successes as permanent. No observed session was conducted; exact priors, stochastic updates, content graph, conversational architecture and voice are unknown. Replicate the principle through a small reviewed prerequisite DAG and transparent uncertainty; reproducing the complete ALEKS learning space would be a substantially larger research undertaking. Pricing varies by product/institution and was not verified here.

## Europe / United States: Area9 Lyceum / Rhapsode

**Distinct contribution:** confidence calibration and metacognition as first-class signals. The official adaptive-learning page describes formative questions, confidence selection for each answer, a refresher (“Recharge”), authoring/analytics and LMS integrations (SCORM/xAPI/LTI). It advertises licensing by user, course or enterprise. “Biological adaptive” is the vendor’s term and does not establish a public mathematical specification, reinforcement learning or superiority over BKT. [G09](https://area9lyceum.com/adaptive-learning/adaptive-learning/)

**Reproduce:** optional confidence input followed by independent calibration, distinguishing confidently wrong misconceptions from uncertain correct guesses. **Unknown:** update equations, prerequisites, experimental comparisons for the relevant school population and Arabic voice. Avoid copying its broad marketing efficacy percentage into the business case; commission a pilot comparison instead. Confidence should adjust evidence collection and feedback, not punish anxious students or override demonstrated performance.

## Middle East: Alef Education

**Distinct contribution:** regional language/content infrastructure and school/government deployment. The official product ecosystem places adaptive pathways, baseline/growth assessment, Abjadiyat native Arabic literacy, Arabits non-native Arabic and Miqyas AlDhad measurement in one portfolio. This is a stronger Arabic content/measurement reference than evidence of Egyptian dialect tutoring. Its described Quantile/Lexile alignment is useful for content difficulty, not proof of Egyptian examination alignment. [G10](https://annualreport.alefeducation.com/the-value-creation-architecture/the-product-ecosystem)

The AI page advertises personalized guidance and diagnostic/predictive functions, plus responsible-AI principles covering reliability, fairness, security and privacy. It does not disclose a named knowledge-tracing model, prompts, orchestration or evaluation implementation. [G11](https://www.alefeducation.com/products/artificial-intelligence)

**Implication:** compare the Arabic literacy/assessment stack and school analytics rather than only chatbot screens. **Unknown:** Arabic dialect/code-switching error rates, voice interruption handling, current per-student commercial price, mastery equations and model-update governance. Vendor outcomes must be checked against study design; system scale is not efficacy evidence. No accounts were accessed.

## Middle East / Egypt: Abwaab

The developer’s App Store listing specifies grades 4–12, country-selected national curricula including Egypt, videos, question banks, instant model answers, timed assessment, progress reporting and Morshed planning/contact via WhatsApp. It advertises learner competition and teacher/student Q&A. This makes it a direct local experience/packaging comparator, with a human-support component. App-store age rating is not the pedagogical target age. The listing establishes free download with in-app purchases, not a verified local subscription quote. [G12](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/abwaab/id1623367993)

No reviewed primary technical publication establishes its persistent knowledge tracing, misconception model, LLM architecture or closed-loop model training. Do not classify it as technically equivalent to ALEKS or Riiid. For the pilot, reproduce useful parent communication and exam-aligned practice through original design; do not adopt competitive leaderboards as a requirement.

## Egypt: an overlooked direct competitor worth a follow-up

Zatoona AI’s own guide (29 October 2026) claims an Egyptian curriculum tutor accepting text/photo/voice, Egyptian Arabic explanations, grade-organized chapters and a small free daily allowance. This is a **vendor claim, untested**, useful to prevent an unsupported assertion that local Arabic AI tutoring is an empty market. No independent evidence or technical architecture was inspected, and its comparison with rivals is interested marketing. [G13](https://zatoona-ai.com/en/guides/ai-apps-egyptian-curriculum)
