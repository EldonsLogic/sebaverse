# Maat — brand-name exploration

11 November 2026. Status: active founder-requested exploration (U6), not final selection or G2 approval. Existing project remains TBD. The founder specifically welcomes tweaks such as **Maat AI** and **The Maat**; focus on this name family rather than unrelated coined names.

## Name family

| Form | Intended character / creative judgment | Practical weakness | Domain candidate, unverified |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Maat AI** | Direct, contemporary technology brand; immediately signals the product type | AI is a generic descriptor and does not distinguish an existing education name by itself | maatai.com; maat.ai |

## Working recommendation, not a decision

Compare **Maat AI**, **The Maat**, and **With Maat** first. Maat AI is the clearest technology descriptor; The Maat has a more editorial identity; With Maat gives the strongest companion voice. Keep “Maat” visually dominant and treat AI/descriptor as secondary where appropriate, but approve the full spoken/written brand rather than assuming the descriptor can disappear. No final preference is assigned to the founder.

## Meaning, pronunciation and writing

The Met describes Maat as embodying right/truth in ancient Egyptian thought: [object record](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/552698). This is a heritage connection, not the literal meaning “AI tutor” or “personalized learning.” Clear understanding, proportion and a calm learning pace are modern creative associations, not ancient definitions.

Use **Maat** as the practical Latin spelling candidate; **Ma’at** more visibly signals the historical reference but adds punctuation friction. Proposed Arabic **ماعت** needs founder/native review alongside **معات**; do not treat either modern rendering or an intended modern brand pronunciation as a recovered ancient pronunciation. Test whether Egyptians retain the middle consonant or hear مات; do not assume a problem or dismiss it without hearing real users. Spoken tests: “بذاكر على ماعت”, “ماعت بيشرحلي”, and “Maat helps me study.” Test both unaided reading and spelling after hearing once.

## Visual directions to compare later

1. Contemporary wordmark: custom bilingual lettering, warm off-white and deep ink blue, restrained spacing; Maat is prominent and AI/descriptor secondary.
2. Editorial learning companion: confident serif/Arabic pairing, muted terracotta and ink, humane illustration; especially suited to The Maat.
3. Abstract balance: an original stable/open geometric symbol and humanist lettering, neutral palette with one accent; modern interpretation of proportion rather than literal justice scales.

These are briefs, not produced logos. Avoid assuming a feather, scales or hieroglyphic symbol is unique or cleared. Final symbol/lettering/slogan follows the preferred name form and similarity review. No museum imagery or competitor marks are copied.
