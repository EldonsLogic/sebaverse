# Sebaverse — The learning workshop / ورشة الفهم

11 November 2026 • Independent Arabic-first brand trial 02 • NOT APPROVED.

## Strategy and promise

Learning is something a student actively constructs. The identity organizes small attempts, explanations and independent checks into visible, manageable blocks.

The student should feel able to take one useful action without pressure; the parent should understand what is evidenced, what remains uncertain and what comes next. Brand quality is deliberate and accessible, not luxury. First use case: German from a learner-uploaded national-language-school curriculum; identity remains multi-subject and age-flexible. No invented age, grade or CEFR level.

## Logo direction

A modular five-direction sign built from five separate wedge-like pieces, with a stable central gap. Abstract N14 structure, not a literal starfish and not an angular S. A rounded but precisely constructed Latin wordmark balances the modular symbol; no exaggerated bubbly letters.

This is an original construction brief. N14 reference: [Thot Sign List](https://thotsignlist.org/mysign?id=4188). Source establishes a star sign; the brand interpretation of guidance/learning is modern. It does not prove that the mark is unique or that Sebaverse is an ancient word. Rejected designs—starfish/coastal, sportswear star-in-A, S/esports monograms—are excluded.

## Color system and measured pairs

Primary **#4B234A**; accent **#FFC84A**; paper **#FFF9EB**; ink **#2D2030**; secondary text **#6D586C**. Approximate use: 70% quiet surfaces, 20% ink/primary, 10% accent maximum. Semantic success/error/warning colors must be separately specified and measured on the actual component; they are not the brand accent.

Ratios are computed from proposed flat sRGB HEX pairs, not sampled from generated images. They do not certify whole-interface compliance. Do not use failed accent pairings for small text. Dark mode candidate: ink surface, paper text, accent for decorative/selected emphasis after checks; do not automatically invert photos or source documents.

## Typography and graphic language

Arabic leads: **Alexandria**. Latin companion: **Manrope**. Final exact files and logo lettering pending.

Rectangular learning blocks, numbered tabs, purposeful cut-paper diagrams and small variable-size modules. Yellow marks a selected action, not every card. Large square corners mixed with restrained 8px inner radii.

Motion: Blocks settle once in 180ms after completion. Use a discreet check and text, not fireworks or punitive broken streaks.

## Verbal identity and copy

Tone: Practical, encouraging and energetic without hype. Make trying again feel ordinary, never a punishment.

Proposed primary slogan: **كل محاولة بتفتح فهم جديد.**

English adaptation: **Understanding takes practice.**

Hero headline: **جرّب. افهم. وجرّب تاني.**

Supporting Arabic copy: هنقسم الدرس لأجزاء صغيرة. تجرّب بنفسك، وتاخد مساعدة وقت ما تحتاجها.

Incorrect response: المحاولة دي ورّتنا الجزء اللي محتاج تدريب. تحب تلميح صغير؟

Independent progress: المرة دي حلّيتها لوحدك. دي خطوة نقدر نبني عليها.

Parent digest: اتدرّبت على تكوين الجملة، وبعد الشرح حلّت مثالًا جديدًا باستقلالية. النشاط القادم يراجع نفس المهارة بصياغة مختلفة.

Alternative slogan proposals: “شرح من مصادرك، وخطوة تناسبك.” / “من السؤال للفهم، خطوة بخطوة.” Do not use all slogans simultaneously.

Main weakness: Modular shapes can become childish or visually busy. Keep decorative elements out of the tutor transcript and assessment prompts.

## Arabic-first identity rules

The public wordmark is always Latin **Sebaverse**. Do not create an Arabic logo or a second Arabic logotype. In Arabic copy, isolate the Latin brand name as LTR; pronounce/test the proposed brand reading with native speakers before standardizing an Arabic explanatory spelling. Logo does not mirror in RTL. Reading flow, main navigation, progress groups and source drawers follow RTL. German sentences, URLs, email, formulas and Latin course identifiers remain LTR in isolated spans. Back/forward UI navigation may mirror; media play icons, actual German text and brand artwork do not. No tracking of joined Arabic letters, no forced kashida for alignment, no reversed numbers. Match Arabic and Latin optically rather than setting identical pixel sizes blindly.

## Type and layout specification

Arabic body 18px/1.8; mobile 17px/1.8; headings 40/1.35 desktop, 30/1.4 mobile; subheading 24/1.5; label 14/1.6; caption 13/1.6, never for essential instructions. Use 400/500/600 weights by role, and test diacritics and German ä ö ü ß. Proposed grid: 12 desktop columns with 24px gutters, mobile 4 columns with 16px gutters; spacing 4/8/12/16/24/32/48/64. Reading measure ~55–70 characters is a starting hypothesis to test for Arabic; not an enforced line break. No justification for conversational content. Minimum proposed touch target 44px; visible focus with contrasting outline; support 200% zoom and reduced motion. Typography choices are candidates, not the exact font used inside generated logo lettering. Final wordmark needs custom vector construction after selection.

Logo primary: wordmark alone or symbol+wordmark; compact: standalone symbol only after recognition testing. Proposed clear space: the wordmark capital S cap height around the whole lockup; minimum 120px horizontal wordmark and 24px symbol pending actual small-size tests. Mono ink, white reversed and approved primary pairings only. Never stretch, bevel, outline decoratively, mirror, rotate, replace letters with unrelated hieroglyphs or use as an answer-correctness badge. Generated raster boards are concept visualizations, not outlined vector masters or font proofs.

## Product applications

1. **RTL landing hero:** Latin Sebaverse in the right-side brand area, Arabic headline and supporting sentence, primary CTA “ابدأ بمنهجك”, secondary “شوف طريقة الشرح”. No claims of guaranteed grades.
2. **Student home:** “نكمل من آخر مرة؟”, current course “الألماني”, next activity “ترتيب الكلمات”, short independent-check queue, visible sources and recording control. Show unknown state as “لسه محتاجين نجرب” rather than zero mastery.
3. **Upload:** “ارفع المنهج أو ورقة التدريب” → “بنجهّز الملف للشرح” → “راجع ترتيب الدروس قبل ما نبدأ”. Private scope and rights declaration are clear, not hidden in branded decoration. Low-confidence OCR: “في جزء مش واضح في الصفحة ٤. محتاجين نسخة أوضح.”
4. **Tutor:** Arabic explanation followed by an isolated LTR German example “Ich lerne Deutsch.” and answer field. Clearly separate hint-assisted practice from an independent check: “السؤال ده من غير تلميحات. تقدر تختار: مش عارف.”
5. **Parent dashboard:** demonstrated skill, uncertainty, next activity and usage. Never a confident percentage derived from chat praise. Keep private chat out of the default summary.
6. **Social:** one Arabic question or explanation, one visual teaching example, one CTA, small Latin logo. Never a collage of slogans/stars.
7. **Icons:** monochrome favicon, light/dark app tile, tiny wordmark test and a low-bandwidth source reference icon. Actual small-size validation occurs after vector refinement.

## Microcopy and tone guardrails

Welcome: “أهلًا! نبدأ بالجزء اللي محتاج مساعدة فيه؟” Resume: “نكمل من آخر خطوة؟” Hint: “تحب تلميح صغير قبل ما تجرب؟” Uncertainty: “المصدر مش واضح كفاية عشان أأكد الإجابة.” Recoverable error: “حصلت مشكلة في تحميل الملف. جرّب تاني، وشغلك محفوظ.” Remaining voice quota: “فاضل ٥ دقايق صوت. تقدر تكمل كتابة بعدها.” No automatic charges or pressure.

Say “رتّبت الجملة صح من غير مساعدة”, not “أنت عبقري”. Say “نجرّب طريقة شرح تانية”, not “ده سهل جدًا”. Do not ridicule mistakes, infer permanent intelligence/learning styles, promise unlimited attention or use fear/shame as motivation. Parent copy favors clear MSA; student copy favors natural Egyptian Arabic without meme/slang overload. Teacher copy is concise, factual and source-linked. Slogans are proposals, not founder-approved or trademark-cleared.

## Imagery, motion and accessibility

Use original subject diagrams, page annotations and learner action rather than stock happy-child photos or staged high-tech classrooms. No unsolicited mascot until exact age positioning is known. Keep visible record/pause/stop controls in voice mode. Feedback uses text plus icon, never color alone; preserve layout during streaming and pauses. Both learning content and branding require Arabic screen-reader, narrow-screen, mixed-direction, long-label and reduced-motion checks. Font choices have official OFL files, but the exact downloaded files/versions and notices must be included at implementation. No fonts are bundled in this trial.
