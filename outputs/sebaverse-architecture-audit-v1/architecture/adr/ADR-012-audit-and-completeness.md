# ADR-012 — Audit handoff and coverage

Status: Selected technical baseline v1 under U11/U12, 11 November 2026; engineering decision for audit, not founder blanket approval.

## Context

Requirements: R001 R002 R003 R081 R089 R099. See technical-baseline.md, associated subject/API/deployment contracts and dated source checks E22–E32.

## Decision

Every requirement has functions/phase/build-reuse/acceptance/source/dependency coverage; all 22 audited repositories receive explicit decisions. Shareable immutable export includes original research/citations, editable diagrams and auditor response template. No external send.

## Alternatives and consequence

Partial summary; silent omission of future requirements; marketing architecture without evidence labels. Selected design keeps ownership and scope testable but requires explicit integration/evaluation work. No runtime results claimed.

## Reversal and execution

Change only through a dated ADR amendment with measured failure/benefit and migration/rights review. Implementation/deployment and real-data permissions remain separate.
