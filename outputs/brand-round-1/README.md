# Brand decision pack — round 1

Status: founder review, not selected or cleared. Source U5. German with learner-uploaded curriculum is the first use case; the identity serves the broader adaptive tutoring platform. Original editable SVG sketches are supplied, with provisional system-font lettering. Arabic spellings and slogans are creative drafts, not finalized lockups.

## Brand brief

Patient and capable. Approachable for students, credible for parents. Promise: understand, apply independently and retain. Avoid German-only positioning, guaranteed marks, generic robot mascots and overly infantile styling. Choose name/direction before polishing final lettering and issuing final brand guidelines.

## Darj / دَرْج

- Slogan: **One clear step forward.**
- Egyptian Arabic proposal: **كل خطوة أوضح.**
- Personality: Confident, structured, purposeful.
- Palette: `#3244A8` primary; `#F0B7A4` accent; warm off-white canvas.
- [Concept board](darj-concept.svg) · [Editable symbol](darj-mark.svg).

Fahmori is a coined name drawing on فهم (understanding); its suffix has no claimed linguistic meaning. Proposed pronunciation: fah-MO-ree. The F-like mark suggests a clear starting point and a separate next step. Risks: English spelling recall, pronunciation, and similarity to other fahm-based names need testing.

Darj uses a step/gradation association; native review must assess its several Arabic meanings. Its staircase mark makes progress literal. Risks: English pronunciation and a short common word's discoverability. Do not describe it as a uniquely educational Arabic word.

My creative preference is **Fahmori** for its clearer understanding association and warm tone; this is not a final selection or a clearance recommendation. Darj is the more restrained, structured alternative. The founder may reject both and request a different naming territory.

## Draft guidelines for the chosen direction

**Purpose and positioning.** Help learners understand their own curriculum, practice independently and remember later. Be specific about sources and evidence, honest about uncertainty. German is a course descriptor under the parent brand.

**Tone.** Warm, concise and respectful. “خلّينا نجرب مثال تاني” offers help without labeling the learner weak. Avoid grade guarantees, exaggerated intelligence claims and emotional dependence. Egyptian Arabic guides conversation, MSA carries formal terms, English is available for explanation, and German is the target subject.

**Logo rules.** Preserve proportions; proposed clear space is one quarter of symbol width; proposed minimum symbol 24px pending small-size review. Produce horizontal/bilingual, symbol-only, mono and reversed versions after selection. No stretching, shadows or low-contrast busy backgrounds. Present SVGs are sketches, not a final bespoke Arabic/Latin wordmark or logo-similarity clearance.

**Color.** Choose one palette, not a blend of both. Accent is decorative until measured for contrast. Use distinct semantic colors and text/icons for status. Verify accessible contrast on actual component pairings before finalizing.

**Typography.** Choose readable Arabic/Latin families with German ä/ö/ü/ß and MSA diacritics; verify exact font license and rendering. Board Arial is a placeholder. Native review is required for the Arabic wordmark, not automatic text substitution.

**Layout.** Calm surfaces, short lesson blocks, generous spacing, clear source/independent-check states. RTL Arabic shell must isolate LTR German examples correctly. Maintain visible focus and accessible error/status messaging.

**Imagery.** Original simple learning paths and subject illustrations; no borrowed competitor assets. Defer mascot choice until age positioning is known. Do not make a generic AI sparkle the central identity.

**Applications.** Test app icon, lesson header, upload confirmation, guardian digest, source card, social avatar and monochrome print. A course badge or German lesson title remains subordinate to the platform brand.

**Claims.** Describe demonstrated independent performance and uncertainty. Avoid first-Arabic-tutor, proven-mastery and exam-gain claims without adequate evidence.
