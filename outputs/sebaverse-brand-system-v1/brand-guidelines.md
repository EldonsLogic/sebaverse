# Sebaverse brand guidelines — v1.0

11 November 2026. Working brand system, editable and versioned. Source U10: founder selects C visual identity + B blue/orange; explicitly does not select “كل سؤال باب” wording. Full G2 remains open. This release authorizes documentation and design assets, not product development.

## Brand foundation

Sebaverse helps learners understand and practise from their curriculum, with explanations that adapt to their responses. Egypt first; Arabic leads, German is the initial learning subject. The first experience starts with an uploaded curriculum. Parent trust comes from visible sources, bounded assistance and honest progress, not claims of guaranteed grades.

Personality: clear, thoughtful, encouraging, lively without noise. Speak to learners respectfully; no baby talk, guilt, artificial intimacy or teacher replacement claims. Explain what the next action achieves. Keep navigation literal: المواد، الدروس، تدريبات، تقدّمي. Doorway is a visual mark, not a naming system for courses or achievement levels.

## Name and logo

Always write the logo **Sebaverse**, Latin, left-to-right, with supplied outlined vector artwork. In Arabic sentences use an isolated Latin name. Do not invent an Arabic logo or translate the name. No mandatory slogan is approved. No “كل سؤال باب”, “الباب اتفتح” or recurring door metaphor in product copy.

The retained C mark uses nested doorway frames and a star. It is a contemporary illustration inspired by the chosen direction; do not describe its conventional star geometry as an exact Egyptian hieroglyph. No trademark or domain clearance is implied.

Use primary color lockup on white or canvas; black for single-color printing; white on solid dark backgrounds. Keep a clear zone equal to the star width around the complete lockup. Proposed minimums: full lockup 160 CSS px or 35 mm; symbol 24 px or 6 mm. At 16 px use a simplified favicon after optical testing; do not shrink full logo. No stretch, rotation, shadows, gradients, extra stars, outlines or replacement type. Compact logo is for tight spaces, symbol for app/avatar only. Preserve frame/star proportions and Latin wordmark spacing. Final optical polish remains an explicit review item.

## Color

Core: blue #2347D6, orange #FF8A3D, ink #151922, canvas #F4F6FA, white #FFFFFF. Secondary: blue-deep #172F96, blue-tint #E4EAFF, orange-tint #FFE8D9, muted #535D71, border #DCE1EB.

Use blue for primary action, selected navigation and focus. Use orange for the star and limited emphasis, with dark ink text. Blue does not mean mastery; orange does not mean error. Use semantic green/red/amber with labels and icons. Default screens are mostly white/canvas, with a small number of high-emphasis actions. Avoid full orange page backgrounds and orange body text. Never use white text on orange. Borders alone are not sufficient input boundaries; use muted border on controls. Dark mode is deferred pending separately tested tokens.

## Typography

Arabic display: Alexandria, weights 600–700. UI/body and Latin/German: Rubik 400–600. Diacritic-heavy passages: Noto Naskh Arabic, with reviewed line height. Use bundled fonts and preserve their OFL files when distributed. Latin logo is outlined artwork, not typed text.

Body 16px minimum; 18px for extended lesson explanations; Arabic line height 1.8. Caption 12px only for optional metadata, not instructions or consent. Labels 14px; headings 24/32/48px with 1.4 line height. At mobile use 32px hero headings. Avoid full justification, tight Arabic tracking, clipped diacritics, forced uppercase and arbitrary ornamental Arabic. Limit lesson reading width to roughly 60–70 characters; allow natural line wrapping and 200% text zoom.

## Voice and wording

Use natural Egyptian Arabic for encouragement and explanations, clear Arabic for consent/settings. Keep German examples in German; do not silently translate the learner’s answer. Ask one useful question at a time. Offer typing as an alternative to speech. Adapt form of address only from chosen preference; default to neutral wording where possible.

Proposed homepage heading: **افهم بطريقتك. واتدرّب على منهجك.** This is draft copy, not an approved permanent slogan. Supporting line: ارفع المنهج، اختار الدرس، وابدأ الشرح والتدريب خطوة بخطوة.

Examples: “نبدأ من أي درس؟” / “إجابتك قريبة. راجع أداة التعريف.” / “الإجابة صح بعد التلميح. نجرب سؤال جديد؟” / “المصدر: الملف المرفوع، صفحة ١٤.” / “مش لاقي إجابة واضحة في الصفحات دي. نراجع المصدر سوا؟”. Avoid “سهل جدًا”، “عبقري”، “خلصت لوحدك” after hints, guaranteed outcomes and fictional teacher availability.

## Imagery and applications

Use curriculum fragments, clear annotation and purposeful geometry. Do not use ancient-Egypt costume imagery, pyramids as decoration, stock robot tutors or athletic star badges. Photos of children require appropriate rights and consent; this kit contains none. Social layouts: Latin logo, one short Arabic message, clear contrast, optional curriculum illustration. Never publish actual learner work or progress by default.

## Governance

Core identity and palette are founder-selected. Copy, spacing, components and accessibility behavior are proposed working specifications for review. Record changes in CHANGELOG.md, bump token version on breaking role changes, keep historical exports immutable. A new color must have a named role and checked text/background pairing. Review desktop/mobile RTL, German strings, long labels, keyboard focus, reduced motion and assisted-progress language before sign-off. No complete accessibility certification or learning efficacy result is claimed.
