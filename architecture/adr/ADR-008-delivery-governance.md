# ADR-008 — Phased delivery and controlled improvement

Status: proposed, not approved. Date: 2026-11-10.

## Context and evidence

Sources: U1;U2;G05;A11. Founder requires affordability, preserved scope, provider independence and controlled implementation. Research findings remain bounded by their stated audit limits.

## Proposed decision

No implementation before G1–G3; synthetic/adult bounded PoC then supervised pilot; signed release lineage and rollback.

## Alternatives

Build all features immediately; autonomous prompt/policy/training updates.

## Rationale and consequences

Solo feasibility and child-facing risk require concrete gates and reviewed evidence. This adds owned domain and review work; it does not eliminate educator/security/operations cost.

## Reversal / validation trigger

Scope/gate changes need explicit decision with unchanged historical requirements.
