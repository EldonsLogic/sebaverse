# ADR-004 — Provider eligibility and routing

Status: proposed, not approved. Date: 2026-11-10.

## Context and evidence

Sources: U2;A09;G06. Founder requires affordability, preserved scope, provider independence and controlled implementation. Research findings remain bounded by their stated audit limits.

## Proposed decision

Qualified adapters; privacy/safety/task quality before budget; default plus fallback; Jev experimental.

## Alternatives

Single proprietary provider deeply embedded; cost-only route; local GPUs first.

## Rationale and consequences

Keeps business/learning authority independent and avoids cheap failure. This adds owned domain and review work; it does not eliminate educator/security/operations cost.

## Reversal / validation trigger

Change route only after benchmark/terms release review; no silent model alias drift.
