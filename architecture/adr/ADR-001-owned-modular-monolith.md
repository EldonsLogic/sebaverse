# ADR-001 — Owned modular monolith

Status: proposed, not approved. Date: 2026-11-10.

## Context and evidence

Sources: A07;A10;A12;G10. Founder requires affordability, preserved scope, provider independence and controlled implementation. Research findings remain bounded by their stated audit limits.

## Proposed decision

Use owned modules and one application domain, restricted parser worker; no forced DeepTutor/LearnHouse merger.

## Alternatives

Adopt full DeepTutor; LMS-first LearnHouse/Moodle; microservices.

## Rationale and consequences

Own evidence/consent/rights without duplicated identities and operational overhead. This adds owned domain and review work; it does not eliminate educator/security/operations cost.

## Reversal / validation trigger

If validated school buyer requires full LMS workflows, reconsider licensed LMS boundary.
