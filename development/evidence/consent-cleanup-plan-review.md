Guardians must have their own eligible consent to access the resource, even when another guardian can still support learner access. A generation change, loss of live requester access or current uploader role is insufficient evidence that a particular upload should be destroyed. ## Conditions for a restricted worker

- A canonical eligible withdrawal and its cleanup intent must commit together, or roll back together. Persist the policy version and exact server-derived target set or equivalent immutable scope and cutoff. Compute basis loss at that transaction's linearization point. Consent activation, withdrawal and the upload allocation boundary need coherent ordering around that point. Store and use `generation < post-withdrawal cutoff` for a broad approved cohort, with the stored tutoring scope and trusted tenant/learner bindings. Bind per-object claims to immutable object identities. Delay or regrant must never expand an old intent to a new object. Avoid taking quota or upload locks while holding the learner lock in the withdrawal transaction. The existing ingestion order is quota, upload, learner. [Ingestion locks and allocation](../../backend/modules/ingestion/service.py#L23-L61).
- Bind the worker to committed intent and its own narrowly scoped internal service operation. Verify target membership and trusted storage root before unlink. The worker may inspect state, cancel eligible writing, unlink fixed object suffixes and settle quota. It needs neither content access nor upload/approval authority. No external caller, stored document text, generic job argument or path may select the objects. Keep the uploader's existing owner check and coordinate its settlement with the cleanup worker.
- Fence source writers and later manifest publication before doing I/O. A stale writer flag or an elapsed claim defeats neither a live descriptor nor a paused attempt. Recover storage ownership only after confirmed process/descriptor closure or an enforced equivalent fence. Otherwise retain durable pending work and held quota with a visible reason. Existing ingress and explicit withdrawal can settle while cleanup is in flight. Both must share the authoritative completion condition and keep late results fenced. A stale or duplicate attempt can have no independent accounting effect. [Ingress and late publication](../../backend/modules/ingestion/service.py#L64-L109) · [Manifest publication](../../backend/modules/ingestion/service.py#L170-L189) · [Existing owner withdrawal](../../backend/modules/ingestion/service.py#L262-L280).
- A partial purge, interrupted loop or lost claim leaves discoverable work. A keyset cursor can advance past an unresolved row only if that object's pending obligation survives restart, or if the final reconciliation deterministically rediscovers every unresolved target. Test an early held or failed row, a page boundary, and a successful later row. Release reserved and used amounts from the upload row under the original quota lock and atomically record the terminal state. Cap automatic attempts and retain a restricted recovery route. Successful unlink confirms current namespace absence. A host-crash durability claim requires the adapter to persist the parent-directory update or provide an equivalent storage guarantee before recording final settlement. Keep transient operational fields distinct from the approved terminal receipt. Public logs and receipts contain no raw data, rights declarations, filenames, content hashes or consent evidence.
- Pin the manifest writer decision in the implementation slice. The current manifest write occurs under lifecycle locks, but the plan's slice table promises no I/O under writer locks. A bounded cleanup can preserve existing manifest serialization and keep cleanup I/O outside transactions. Moving the existing write requires explicit durable ownership, quota and stale-writer coverage. Both approaches must demonstrate that no eligible object can acquire a writer after the withdrawal fence.

## Final decision recheck

**Accepted for founder decision.** The [choice memo](consent-cleanup-founder-choice.md) accurately treats overlapping grants as a choice between an effective-loss rule and learner-wide cleanup after any qualifying tutoring withdrawal. Both exclude duplicate, ineffective and reporting-only withdrawals. Full purge removes the original and manifest, while manifest-only purge keeps the accepted original inaccessible and charged. Approved retention purpose, disposition timing and access for any retained original, and the minimum retained intent, upload and consent/audit fields and lifetimes, remain outstanding. The revised plan preserves manifest serialization, keeps cleanup I/O outside transactions, tracks unresolved targets across sweeps and requires the storage adapter’s deletion durability to be qualified before terminal credit. Align the acceptance matrix with those conditions, including recovery across a page boundary. Implementation, runtime acceptance, R046 closure and F3 exit remain pending.
