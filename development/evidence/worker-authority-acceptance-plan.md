# DEV-010 production worker authority acceptance plan

23 November 2026 · R039/R040/R041/R046 · RISK-F3-004. Source inspected at `d8a1509`. An incorrect handoff could expose a learner's source, let an untrusted worker clear its own scan, or retain private bytes after withdrawal. The present scope is one design and acceptance record using authored-fixture examples. All cases below are proposed and **NOT RUN**. ## Current source and gaps

- The authored broker creates a new dispatch under quota → upload → learner locks, reads and hashes the selected object, invokes the scanner, checks authority in a transaction and calls the parser outside it. It rechecks before writing the manifest. Neither engine call has a single-use source release ordered against revocation. Consent withdrawal takes the learner lock and bumps authorization generation. Live access also depends on actor, membership, tenant, learner, relationship or assignment, qualifying consent, enrollment, entitlement and resource/course/edition facts. Reading those facts does not prove that their mutators share the same fence. Natural expiry needs a trusted clock check at the boundary. [Broker locks and scope](../../backend/modules/ingestion/service.py#L20) · [Dispatch and result](../../backend/modules/ingestion/service.py#L165) · [Live access](../../backend/modules/access/service.py#L9) · [Consent fence](../../backend/modules/identity/consent.py#L35)
- The broker's scanner adapter replies with an asset identifier, hash and status. Default cleared assets are empty. The separate authored PDF core checks exact job and clean-scan envelopes with fixture HMAC keys. Its expiry window is relative to verification, and the fixture does not establish a dispatch-wide deadline, one-time release or production key provisioning. The stdin fixture and watchdog establish bounded authored process transport. They do not demonstrate a sealed two-engine handoff, isolation across jobs, independent cleanup after broker failure, or whole-tree termination. ## Acceptance matrix

| Correction | Offline contract fixtures when eligible | Required database or runtime evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Release and revocation ordering | Drive both scanner and parser through deterministic barriers immediately before and after the engine's input becomes usable. If cancellation, expiry or revocation wins first, the pending release is invalidated and the engine receives zero readable source capabilities. Pause after reservation or ticket issuance but before actual release to catch a delayed or queued send. If release wins first, stale authority is recorded before teardown and subsequent output is discarded. A stop request or an available descriptor name is insufficient as the test oracle. | Use independent database connections and a qualified handoff. Report the lock/fence mapping for each supported mutator. Cover consent withdrawal/regrant, upload withdrawal/delete, grant or relationship loss, expiry, membership/actor/tenant/learner changes, enrollment or allowance loss, and resource or course/edition drift. Unsupported invalidators must fail closed before enablement. Compare both race orders, including expiry and revocation immediately before final acceptance. Assert regrant cannot revive the old dispatch. Assert staging, scanning, parsing, waits and reap occur outside long database lock sections. Record when the real child can first read. Already delivered bytes require bounded teardown. |
| 2. Duplicate delivery, replay and expiry | Freeze broker issue time and a single dispatch deadline. Reject a delayed authentic envelope whose expiry falls outside that original deadline even if close to verifier time. Check the earliest deadline at each release and final acceptance, including equality and waiting for a lock. Run concurrent duplicate verdict and result deliveries, and swap valid envelopes/results between objects or dispatches. Check exact eight-field request binding, six-field result provenance and bounded decoding before hashing signed fields. | Use transactions/barriers around concurrent consumers and process restart. Observe one release per engine for the winning dispatch, one successful manifest decision and one accounting mutation, including lost acknowledgments or duplicate delivery after outcome commit. A retry after expiry or ambiguous failure must use a fresh dispatch, scan and handoff, with the previous release fenced. Verify clock behavior under delayed verification and bounded monotonic elapsed time for staging, engine and cleanup work. |
| 3. Scan signer and measured immutable input | Check approved scanner identity against an exact runtime, signature-data/rules and scan-policy identity. The signer independently measures bounded length and digest on the staged source and binds the authenticated completed verdict to that source and dispatch. Reject wrong job fields, parser-supplied clearance, key-role crossover, wrong/retired signer, key expiry, mismatched digest/count and dirty, partial, skipped, timed-out or indeterminate scans. A valid job signature alone cannot substitute for a scan verdict. | Inspect credential delivery and actual child boundaries for both engines. The trusted endpoints must be identifiable. With HMAC, the broker can also mint scan attestations and remains a trusted verifier. Neither native engine receives keys, credentials or a lookup mechanism. Demonstrate that scanner and parser can read the same verified sealed input through independently positioned read-only handles, with expected size, identity and access mode checked. Reject replacement, incomplete seals, writable aliases or mappings, wrong offsets and extraneous descriptors. Confirm effective isolation from peer jobs, private mounts, control sockets, learner DB and egress. |
| 4. Handle and crash ownership | Drive timeout, cancellation, pipe/launch failure and crash boundaries before handoff, after handoff before acknowledgment, and after output or an artifact write before committed acceptance. Assert an owner outside the failed invocation has an exact cleanup obligation and an idempotent reconciliation path. Never give credit or mark cleanup complete while an owned stage/partial file can survive. Walk the normal and stale paths to confirm only current valid output can reach `needs_review`; rights/extraction approval remains separate. | Terminate only authorized authored test processes. In qualified composition, demonstrate bounded stop, whole-tree reap or confirmed extinction, handle closure and staged/partial-file disposition on normal exit, cancellation and broker/launcher failure. Include an ambiguous handoff and an interrupted artifact write or database rollback. Prevent another invocation from reusing old handles or staged bytes during recovery. Check staging copies, swap/dump/diagnostic policy, surviving artifact charges or exact cleanup obligations, and once-only settlement. Verify no private payload or credential enters logs or crash artifacts. |

Record observed release, reads, acceptance, owner and settlement as ordered test events using synthetic identities and content-free counters. Assertions must distinguish a rejected result from a handoff that never occurred. Preserve the existing signed SDP1 fixture and default-disabled PDF factory while evaluating any revised trusted boundary. A successful authored case should reach only `needs_review`.
