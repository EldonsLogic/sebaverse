- The receiver acquires a shared guard before committing its receiving marker and rechecks eligibility under the lifecycle locks. A delayed receiver that resumes after withdrawal fails before opening the blob. A receiver that has published the marker still holds the guard before opening and throughout streaming and matching acknowledgment. It creates the blob and unlinks a partial write relative to the pinned directory descriptor. Hash setup precedes source open, and the raw source descriptor closes in `finally` even if stream wrapping fails. The guard closes without an explicit unlock that could release an inherited open file description. Receiver failure cleanup acquires a separate exclusive guard after releasing its shared guard.
- Active recovery requires the exact persisted object and attempt, start generation, withdrawal generation, protocol, namespace, root and lock inode. The adapter checks the actual opened stripe against its pathname after acquiring the lock. Legacy, missing, substituted, mismatched and unavailable proofs leave the row and quota intact. The same applies while a compatible process holds the guard even if both names are absent. The fixed 64 stripes and namespace marker contain no upload identifiers and survive purge. A collision can postpone a legitimate purge, including ordinary purge in the opted-in adapter.
- The consent consumer locks quota, upload, learner and intent in the existing order, commits the withdrawal fence, then attempts exclusive acquisition outside the transaction. It verifies the bound target, original cohort and current lease again before filesystem purge and before accounting. Manual recovery uses existing owner authority and also rechecks the exact fenced writer before purge and settlement. Confirmed original and manifest absence and current authority are required to clear the active marker and settle quota. A stale lease after filesystem confirmation leaves the marker and charge for replay. Tests exercise the pre-open fence, abrupt child exit, inherited lock references, stale binding and lease, partial purge, scope isolation and ordinary withdrawal regressions.
