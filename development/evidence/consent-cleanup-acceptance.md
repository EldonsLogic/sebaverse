# DEV-010 consent cleanup acceptance

21 November 2026 · F3 · R046 with R037, R038 and R040 · [RISK-F3-005](../../registers/decisions-risks-unknowns.md#L123) open. These are proposed acceptance cases for an authorized future implementation. All are **NOT RUN**. The risk is stale access, retained private bytes or deletion of someone else's upload. This review is limited to existing source and authored-fixture acceptance because this checkpoint uses synthetic data.

## Baseline and policy decisions

- Canonical withdrawal checks the consent's guardian and tenant membership, then records withdrawal and advances a learner-wide authorization generation once. Uploads pin a learner/course/purpose/edition and generation. They do not pin a particular consent record. The live guard filters consent by resource purpose and, for guardians, by the acting guardian. A generation change can fence unrelated work, so it cannot establish purge authority by itself. [Consent](../../backend/modules/identity/consent.py#L8-L21) · [Upload binding](../../backend/modules/ingestion/service.py#L18-L61) · [Live authorization](../../backend/modules/access/service.py#L19-L47)
- The uploader command uses an authenticated, active owner. It durably denies access before purging, waits while the receiver owns the writer and releases stored and reserved bytes after successful purge. The local adapter unlinks the source then the manifest and ignores files already absent. The existing probe covers explicit uploader deletion, a closed writer with a purge error and a retained partial manifest with a single refund on retry. It still calls uploader withdrawal separately following canonical consent withdrawal. [Broker](../../backend/modules/ingestion/service.py#L262-L280) · [Storage](../../backend/modules/ingestion/storage.py#L60-L62) · [Existing cases](../../backend/tests/ingestion/postgres_probe.py#L126-L173) · [Canonical withdrawal during scan](../../backend/tests/ingestion/postgres_probe.py#L184-L208) · [Canonical withdrawal during read](../../backend/tests/ingestion/postgres_probe.py#L234-L248) · [Partial manifest case](../../backend/tests/ingestion/postgres_probe.py#L393-L434)
- DEV-009 offers examples of durable commands, fenced attempts and retries. Its current job entry points require live user access and a matching generation, reserve synthetic inference units and issue mock provider attempts. Expired running jobs move to unknown billing. Consent cleanup needs to continue after access is denied, with storage accounting independent of provider receipts. [Operations enqueue and claim](../../backend/modules/operations/service.py#L47-L91) · [Expiry recovery](../../backend/modules/operations/service.py#L148-L155)
- Confirm which uploads are eligible when another verified guardian's tutoring consent remains valid, including uploads created by a learner or the other guardian under overlapping grants. Confirm the disposition and timing for original bytes versus derived artifacts, the approved minimal receipt fields and their retention, and any applicable restore obligations. R046 requires purging in-scope derived artifacts. The uploader command's deletion of both files does not settle consent-driven policy. G3 owns the decision. ## Acceptance cases

Use canonical withdrawal and the future cleanup entry point. Stage authored source and manifest markers and record fresh database state, file presence, per-upload counters and aggregate quota. Use independent database connections with explicit barriers for lock-order races, and a restartable authored writer for crash cases on a qualified PostgreSQL runtime.

| Case | Set up and pass condition |
|---|---|
| Authorized trigger and scope | Try unauthenticated, inactive, wrong-guardian and wrong-tenant requests against a known consent, and roll back a valid withdrawal. None can leave durable cleanup work or cause file removal. An arbitrary upload ID or altered scope cannot authorize deletion through the worker. A committed, eligible tutoring withdrawal denies old learner resolution and reviewer reads before any cleanup worker runs. Replay the consent withdrawal and verify one generation bump and one durable cleanup intent. Withdraw reporting consent separately and verify no tutoring deletion follows from the shared generation change. Include other learners/tenants and a same-byte distractor. Their object paths and accounting stay intact. Exercise multiple courses and editions, overlapping grants from the same and different guardians, and expired or otherwise ineffective records. Assert the approved policy's exact candidate set, and check storage presence and access separately for excluded old uploads because a generation fence can still deny reads. Cleanup of eligible objects succeeds even if the original uploader is now inactive. |
| Delayed delivery after regrant | Capture the server-derived target scope and generation cutoff inside the locked withdrawal transaction. Race an allocation with that transaction and assert its inclusion or exclusion against the committed order and approved policy. Then delay cleanup, grant fresh consent as a new record, and allocate and publish a new upload under the same learner/course using a new command key and identical bytes. Deliver the old cleanup command twice. It must honor the original cutoff even after later generation changes. The old upload stays fenced, the new upload remains readable and charged, and the eligible old artifacts are removed as policy requires. Replay the old allocation key and confirm it cannot create a fresh writable session for the old object. |
| Open and abandoned writer | Pause ingress with an open descriptor after a partial write, withdraw, and run cleanup. Pending work and reservation remain durable. Resume the writer and prove it closes without committing the source, then allow confirmed purge and one release. In a separate case terminate the fixture writer before it can clear its flag and restart cleanup. Require recovery based on confirmed writer end or an equivalent enforced fence. While ownership is uncertain, keep the work visible and charged. |
| Partial purge and accounting | Stage a charged partial manifest and let source unlink succeed before manifest unlink fails. Verify denied access, an observable retryable state, no completed receipt and conserved quota while cleanup is incomplete. Retry with source already absent. Separately interrupt persistence after manifest bytes are written but before the charge commits. Verify cleanup removes the uncharged derived file as well. Release only the charges recorded for the upload, once. Confirm unrelated reservations and used bytes are preserved. For any policy-approved retained original, verify the approved accounting and reported disposition separately. |
| Crash and retry | Stop after withdrawal commits but before any worker claim, then again after successful unlink but before terminal state and quota settlement commit. Restart against persisted state. Work is discovered without another guardian command. Already-absent objects complete safely without issuing a second refund. Exhaust bounded automatic retries on a persistent fixture error and confirm failure remains visible, access remains fenced and an authorized retry can finish once the fault clears. |
| Duplicate delivery and late publication | Race two cleanup attempts and the uploader's withdrawal, using independent sessions. Interleave a stale scan/parser result and an approval attempt after the withdrawal cutoff. Verify no source or derived artifact reappears, no stale result becomes reviewable or publishable, quota is released at most once and there is one terminal outcome per eligible target. Retry or cancellation of a cleanup attempt must leave pending work resumable with access still denied. |
| Evidence and retention | Distinguish committed denial, pending or failed cleanup, and confirmed disposition. Confirm physical file absence for each in-scope local object and test all implemented derivative stores, including indexes or caches when present. Preserve only the approved restricted receipt fields needed to show scope, outcome and timing. Assert logs and receipts contain no raw content, snippets, filenames, rights declarations or consent evidence payload. Verify receipt access and expiry against approved rules. Exercise replay after restore when backup handling is in scope. |

Rerun the existing uploader-withdrawal regressions and the F3 suites named by `python3 tools/brief.py DEV-010` against the final implementation. Record the exact source, runtime, fixture and actual result, including independent review. The current fixture storage covers only the source and manifest. Future stores and policy-dependent cases need explicit evidence before they can be marked complete.

Independent-review boundary case for the crash/retry group: place an unresolved active writer before a keyset page boundary and eligible closed objects after it. After restart and later writer closure, verify the early object is revisited and later objects were not starved; neither a cursor advance nor exhausted retry may mark the intent complete while any target remains unresolved. Cleanup file operations stay outside transactions; existing manifest write serialization is preserved until separately redesigned.
