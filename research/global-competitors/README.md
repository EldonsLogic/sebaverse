## Evidence rules

- **D:** directly inspected public source/code or observable behavior. Reading a product page directly observes its claims, not its pedagogical effectiveness.
- **P:** published technical disclosure or research; deployment scope and publication date matter.
- **V:** vendor statement, not independently established.
- **I:** inference supported by evidence; not confirmation of private architecture.
- **H:** proposed design or testable hypothesis for the founder.
- **U:** unknown or not verified. Absence of evidence is not evidence of absence.
- Confidence **high / medium / low** concerns the stated claim, not the entire provider. Algorithm identification requires a technical disclosure, not generic words such as adaptive or AI.
