# 12 — Executive brief

**Decision:** plan an assessment-led tutor for one Egyptian curriculum slice, combining reviewed learning content, explicit student evidence and bounded conversation. Do not begin with neural tracing, reinforcement learning, model self-training or multiple autonomous agents. This recommendation is a design judgment informed by the evidence pack; local learning gains and economics still need measurement.

The global leaders offer complementary ideas rather than one reproducible winning stack. Study diagnosis, prerequisite-sensitive sequencing, independent assessment, delayed retrieval and constrained conversational support as separate capabilities. The strongest reusable advantage is a reliable learning loop whose decisions can be explained and corrected.

## Findings that change decisions

- REA is useful for authorized client/artifact inspection but cannot recover private hosted learning intelligence. It was source-reviewed, not installed: this environment's runtime is unsupported and direct repository fetching failed. [03](03-reverse-engineering-findings.md)
- Published effectiveness evidence must be separated from product claims, immediate assisted success and next-answer prediction. A guarded tutor is not automatically proven to improve broad exam performance. The high-school guardrails study also shows why unaided assessment matters. [T01](11-sources-and-evidence.md)
- Public disclosures support several reproducible mechanisms; exact private mastery equations and current deployment details often remain unknown. [02](02-competitor-deep-dives.md), [04](04-adaptive-learning-engine.md)
- Duolingo's November 2026 packaging transition makes older Max comparisons stale. [A04](11-sources-and-evidence.md)
- Egyptian Arabic voice is a validation opportunity, not an uncontested market gap. Curriculum accuracy, child speech recovery and parent trust must be demonstrated locally. [08](08-differentiation-opportunities.md)

## Ten capabilities worth building, in order

1. Versioned curriculum/objective mapping and authorized content provenance.
2. Original diagnostic items linked to plausible misconceptions.
3. Persistent learner evidence with uncertainty and assistance tracking.
4. Independent checks after support and after delay.
5. Small reviewed prerequisite graph with explainable activity selection.
6. Bounded tutor dialogue with reliable mathematical checks.
7. Arabic/English explanations and measured Egyptian voice interaction.
8. Human-reviewed improvement, regression evaluations and rollback.
9. Minimal parent digests and teacher correction/override.
10. Per-session cost, latency and reliability measurement.

Use original engineering with candidate components such as pyBKT, FSRS and symbolic checking when their measured value justifies them. Public code is not permission to reuse every linked dataset; content and dataset licenses remain separate. [09](09-reproducible-technical-patterns.md)

## Closed-loop design

**Student adaptation:** update individual state from valid evidence. **System improvement:** review aggregated failures and release versioned content/policy changes. **Model improvement:** optional offline training on authorized, curated data with independent evaluation. Keep these three loops separately controlled; none requires autonomous self-training. [05](05-closed-loop-intelligence.md)

## Open decisions and next phase

Resolve the first grade/subject/syllabus, educator capacity, material rights, child-data handling, safeguarding ownership and cost ceiling in architecture planning. Validate independent retention, dialect speech, curriculum mapping, review workload and heavy-use unit economics before broad expansion.

The future repository audit should prioritize assessment-to-mastery integrity, retrieval isolation, content provenance, voice cancellation, release rollback and deletion across stored artifacts. No product repository was supplied, so no code-audit findings are claimed. The structured handoff and ranked acceptance gates are in [10](10-recommendations.md).

This assignment produced research and planning recommendations only; no tutoring product, paid account or production system was created or modified.
