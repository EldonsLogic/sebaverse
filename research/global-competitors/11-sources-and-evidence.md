## Interpretation and exclusions

Source dates are publication metadata where verified, otherwise labeled approximate/unknown. Research disclosures, operational manuals, marketing and search-only leads have different evidentiary strength. Do not count repeated URLs as independent corroboration. Patents were not used to infer deployment. No paid full texts, access restrictions or rate limits were bypassed. Search-only/unreadable leads are explicitly non-confirmatory. Local product testing, independent benchmarking, a comprehensive patent landscape and legal clearance remain outside the evidence actually collected.
